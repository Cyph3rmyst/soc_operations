## What This Project Is About

Comprehensive security investigation documentation brings together all your SOC Analyst skills. Real investigations require log analysis, network forensics, malware examination, and clear reporting. Creating end-to-end investigation documentation proves you can handle actual SOC responsibilities. Well-written reports with clear formatting, proper grammar, and logical organization impress employers — poor documentation suggests poor attention to detail regardless of technical skill.

**What you will learn:**
- Conducting structured security investigations
- Correlating evidence from multiple sources
- Building attack timelines
- Writing investigation reports for different audiences

## Action Steps

1. **Select a challenge or scenario** from one of the platforms above.
2. **Document your initial observations and develop investigation hypotheses.**
3. **Collect and analyze evidence** from available log sources.
4. **Identify the attack vector, compromised systems, and attacker actions.**
5. **Build a complete timeline** showing how the attack progressed.
6. **Write a formal investigation report** with findings and recommendations.

### Investigation report structure

- **Incident Summary** — brief description of what happened
- **Scope** — systems and time period examined
- **Methodology** — tools and techniques used
- **Timeline of Events** — chronological sequence of attacker actions
- **Evidence Analysis** — detailed examination of collected artifacts
- **Root Cause** — how the initial compromise occurred
- **Impact Assessment** — what damage resulted from the incident
- **Recommendations** — steps to prevent similar incidents
- **Appendices** — supporting evidence, IOCs, technical details

### Tips for effective documentation

- Use timestamps consistently throughout your report
- Include screenshots as evidence for key findings
- Explain your reasoning, not just your conclusions
- Write for readers who may not have investigated the incident themselves
- Provide clear, actionable recommendations based on your findings
