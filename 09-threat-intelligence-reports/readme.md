## What This Project Is About

Threat intelligence involves collecting, analyzing, and sharing information about cyber threats. SOC Analysts use threat intelligence to understand attacker tactics and improve detection capabilities. Proactive defense is becoming standard practice — SOC teams that understand current threats can detect attacks faster than teams that only react to alerts. Creating reports demonstrates analytical thinking and research skills.

**What you will learn:**
- Researching threat actors and their methods
- Collecting indicators of compromise from various sources
- Analyzing attack patterns and trends
- Communicating threat information clearly

## Number of Deliverables

- **3–5 threat intelligence reports** on different threats, published on LinkedIn or a personal blog.
- Each report must be structured with all 8 sections listed below.

---

## Action Steps

1. **Choose a recent threat actor or malware family** to research — examples from the source: LockBit ransomware, APT29, Emotet.
2. **Collect information from multiple sources:** vendor reports, news articles, and threat intelligence platforms.
3. **Map the threat to MITRE ATT&CK techniques** to understand attack methods.
4. **Compile indicators of compromise (IOCs):** IP addresses, domains, file hashes.
5. **Write a structured threat intelligence report** with actionable recommendations.
6. Repeat for 3–5 different threats.

### Sections to include in your report

- **Executive Summary** — brief overview for non-technical readers
- **Threat Overview** — background on the threat actor or malware
- **Attack Timeline** — when the threat emerged and evolved
- **Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs)** — how the threat operates
- **Indicators of Compromise** — technical indicators for detection
- **Affected Industries** — which sectors are targeted
- **Detection Recommendations** — how to identify the threat in your environment
- **Mitigation Strategies** — steps to protect against the threat

---

## What to Produce / How to Showcase

- A repo like `Threat-Intelligence-Reports` with one report per threat (PDF/Markdown).
- Include an IOC table (type, value, source, notes) and a MITRE ATT&CK technique mapping table.
- Share on LinkedIn or a personal blog, and join threat-intelligence sharing communities for feedback.
